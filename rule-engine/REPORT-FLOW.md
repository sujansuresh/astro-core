## The four presentation rulings (Machan, 2026-10-31)

1. **Combined reading** — the Moon is primary ("the one making this transit worth").
   The lagna is used **only where cusps are arrived**. One combined narrative, not two
   parallel columns.
2. **Show everything, rank by relevance** — the report lists ALL dated contacts of the
   window; the ones touching the asked domain get highlighted (bold/color), the rest
   stay visible in normal weight. The reader sees the full sky, with the answer marked.
3. **Degree-based intensity (ORB-02)** — Rahu begins to act **3° before** reaching a
   natal planet, is at maximum at the exact crossing, and from the moment it crosses
   (even by 1') the effect starts declining, **completely gone 2.5° past**. Every
   reading and every timeline phrase respects this rise-peak-fade shape
   (≈ 57 days rising, peak, ≈ 47 days fading, ≈ 104 days total per contact).
4. **Report tone (REPORT-01, already locked)** — positives lead (~80%); risks are said
   softly, as possibilities with a caution note — never as fixed doom.

## The seven steps

### Step 1 — Where does the axis fall? (Moon primary, lagna where cusps apply)

Locate the two signs the nodes are transiting and read them **from the Moon first**;
bring in the lagna for the house-cusp matters.

*Sample chart*: Rahu in Makaram = **on the Moon's own sign (1st from Moon)**; Ketu in
Katakam = **7th from Moon**. From the lagna the same axis is 4th/10th. Both frames
fire, so this transit reads double-strength for her: the headline is
**"the transit sits on her birth-sign axis — home base and public standing both move."**

### Step 2 — How strong is the landlord of the transited sign?

A node delivers through the owner of the sign it occupies. Judge that owner's dignity
and placement.

*Sample chart*: Makaram's lord is **Saturn, sitting in his own sign inside the
stellium** — the strongest possible host; Rahu's work this window is delivered with
Saturn's structure behind it. Katakam's lord is **the Moon — who is herself the first
planet Rahu will touch** → Ketu's side of the axis is narrated by a landlord in churn.

### Step 3 — Which natal planets will be touched, and when? (dated contact list)

List every natal planet sitting in the two transited signs, in the order the backward-
walking node will meet them, with each contact's **rise–peak–release dates (ORB-02)**.
Also check: is this window a Rahu return (~18.6 years) or half-return (~9.3 years)?

*Sample chart* — four contacts, all on the Rahu side (Katakam is empty, so Ketu has
none — RKT-03: Ketu's leg reads mildly, at house level only):

| Touch | Rise begins | Exact peak | Fully released |
|---|---|---|---|
| Rahu × **Moon** (mind, mother · 10th lord) | ≈ 14-Nov-2026 | **09-Jan-2027** | ≈ 26-Feb-2027 |
| Rahu × **Venus** (comfort, alliance · lagna & 8th lord) | ≈ 04-Mar-2027 | **29-Apr-2027** | ≈ 15-Jun-2027 |
| Rahu × **Saturn** (structure, grind · yogakaraka landlord) | ≈ 30-Apr-2027 | **25-Jun-2027** | ≈ 11-Aug-2027 |
| Rahu × **Mars** (drive, property · 2nd & 7th lord, exalted) | ≈ 19-Jun-2027 | **14-Aug-2027** | ≈ 01-Oct-2027 |

The four windows nearly join end-to-end: this is **one continuous ~11-month contact
season with four dated peaks**, not four separate events. No return or half-return
falls in this window. *(This ordering + season-stitching is formalized as **RKT-06** —
chronological multi-planet aggregation; the narrative keeps each contact's own dates
and significance while telling one continuous story. Hand-offs between adjacent
contacts are narrated as fading-current + approaching-current progressions — **RKT-07**.)*

*(Note: the Moon's rise begins ≈ 23 days before Rahu even enters Makaram — the 3°
approach crosses the sign border. Machan ruled 2026-10-31 that the degree curve is
**sign-boundary independent** (ORB-02 clause; GK-12 resolved) — the rise reads from
the border-crossing date, in the mild/early-indication register.)*

### Step 4 — Walk the transit star by star (sub-periods)

Inside the sign, the node crosses 2¼ stars. Each star colors its months with its
lord's nature; stars ruled by the nodes themselves run one notch louder.

*Sample chart* (Makaram, backward order): **Avittam** (Mars's star — her own birth
star: the transit opens "at home") 07-Dec-2026 → 11-Apr-2027 · **Thiruvonam** (Moon's
star — hosts the Saturn and Mars peaks) 11-Apr-2027 → 19-Dec-2027 · **Uthiradam**
(Sun's star — authority-textured tail, no more touches) 19-Dec-2027 → 25-Jun-2028.
None of the three is a node-ruled star → no extra loudening this window.

*Where a star leg carries no direct natal contact, the leg is governed by its
nakshatra lord — natal + transit channels, dignity and the 50% debilitation rider:
**RKT-08**. Inter-contact gaps are never blank: they read as star-lord-mediated
connective tissue — **RKT-09**.*

### Step 5 — What life-period is running alongside? (dasha check)

A transit shouts loudest where the running period agrees with it. Check the running
Maha Dasha and Bhukti lords against (a) the axis houses and (b) the planets being
touched — **if the running period lord IS the planet being touched, that touch peaks
(RKT-02)**.

*Sample chart*: Jupiter Maha Dasha throughout. Bhuktis: **Moon till 11-Apr-2027** →
the Moon touch (peak 09-Jan-2027) lands inside the Moon's own bhukti — the opening
surge is double-marked. **Mars 11-Apr-2027 → 17-Mar-2028** → the Mars touch
(peak 14-Aug-2027) is likewise double-marked — the report's single strongest dated
surge. From Mar-2028 the Rahu bhukti begins → everything Rahu does amplifies.

### Step 6 — The question gate (transit × period on the asked houses)

Now, and only now, filter everything through the asked question. Each question maps to
its houses (kelvigal catalog). The answer = how the transiting axis + the running
period together treat those houses → a band: **Favorable / Mixed / Challenging**, with
the reasons named.

*Sample chart, live verdicts*:
- **Promotion (career)** → **Favorable** — axis on 1/7-from-Moon with the 10th-lord
  Moon touched inside her own bhukti, yogakaraka landlord hosting.
- **Finances** → **Favorable** — exalted 2nd-lord Mars touched at peak during his own
  bhukti.
- **Mental peace** → **Mixed** — the Moon is strengthened by role (digbala, 10th lord)
  yet Rahu churns the mind itself; peace fluctuates while the base builds.
- **Marriage/relationships** → **Favorable** — Venus (alliance) and Mars (7th lord)
  both receive strong touches.
- **Foreign travel** → **Mixed** — the pull exists, but full period-support arrives
  only from the Rahu bhukti (Mar-2028).
- **Speculation** → **Challenging in early 2027** — the surge runs through asset/vault/
  alliance channels, not the 5th; the gate is deliberately not lifted.

### Step 7 — Assemble the report

Stitch in fixed order: axis headline (step 1) → landlord note (2) → dated contact
calendar with the asked-domain touches highlighted (3) → star-by-star flow (4) →
period agreement (5) → the verdict for the asked question with reasons (6) — all
written in the locked tone (positives lead; risks as soft possibilities), with every
date phrase following the rise–peak–release curve (step 3).
