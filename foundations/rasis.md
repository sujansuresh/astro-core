## How to read a rasi entry

Each rasi carries, in fixed order:

1. **Data line** — Sanskrit + Tamil name, zodiac span, lord, element (tattva), quality
   (chara/sthira/dwisvabhava), purushartha group, gender, varna, kala-purusha body zone,
   rising type, direction. Pure data; this is what the engine reads. **Lord + element +
   quality feed factors A/C, DIGN-01, and FUNC-01 — if it's not here, dispositor logic
   cannot run.**
2. **Texture** — how this rasi *feels* and delivers.
3. **Good characteristics** (well-placed placements here) — bullet bank.
4. **Bad characteristics** (afflicted placements here) — bullet bank.
5. **Karakatvas** — numbered signification list (body, places, matters, objects,
   animals, professions, colors, substances). Cite corrections by rasi + number
   (e.g., "Makaram #9").
6. **Dignity & lordship map** — who owns it, who exalts/debilitates here (with deep
   point), whose moolatrikona, the lord's friends/enemies entering it. This is the
   DIGN-01/STRENGTH-01 lookup table in prose-data form.
7. **Transit note** — what a slow transit (esp. the nodes) through this rasi delivers;
   the rasi-flavor bank for factor A.
8. **Worked example** — only for rasis occupied in the sample chart (Thulam, Makaram,
   Dhanus, Mithunam, Simmam, Kumbam, Meenam — 7 of 12; full list in footer).

**Conventions**: whole-sign houses (locked); sidereal; node exaltation/debilitation
**commentary only, never scored** (decision H). Where parambariya schools disagree,
the item is tagged **[school note]** for Machan's ruling.

**Element groups**: fire = Mesham/Simmam/Dhanus (dharma) · earth = Vrishabham/Kanni/
Makaram (artha) · air = Mithunam/Thulam/Kumbam (kama) · water = Katakam/Vrichikam/
Meenam (moksha). Quality cycle: chara (movable) → sthira (fixed) → dwisvabhava (dual),
repeating four times.

**Rising types**: shirshodaya (head-first) = Mithunam, Simmam, Kanni, Thulam,
Vrichikam, Kumbam · prishtodaya (back-first) = Mesham, Vrishabham, Katakam, Dhanus,
Makaram · ubhayodaya (both) = Meenam.

**Kala-purusha body zones** (head → feet): Mesham head · Vrishabham face/neck ·
Mithunam arms/shoulders/lungs · Katakam chest/heart/stomach · Simmam upper belly ·
Kanni intestines/hip · Thulam lower abdomen/groin · Vrichikam genitals/excretory ·
Dhanus thighs · Makaram knees · Kumbam calves/ankles · Meenam feet.
