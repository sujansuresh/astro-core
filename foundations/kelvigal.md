## How to read a question card

```
### C2 — Will I get promoted in this window?
- Kelvikkana bhavagangal : core {10,11} → +2 income jump · +9 authority recognition
- Viruddha bhavangal     : {9}  (12th from primary 10 — dissolves position)
- Kelvikkana kaaraka graham : Sun (authority/status) · Jupiter (elevation grace)
- How arrived            : 10 IS position; 11 = gains arriving; if it doesn't show in
                           pay it isn't a promotion → +2 as flavor.
```

**Core** = must be supported (DBAS × transit, per GATE-01) for a YES.
**Modifiers (+)** = each signified modifier house injects its flavor clause — this is how the
report gets *precise* instead of generic. More flavors get appended here as Machan dictates them.
**Viruddha** = houses that dissolve/spoil the matter; if these are signified strongly while
core support is weak, the answer tilts NO.

## Global mechanics (working rules — Machan to refine from practice)

- **VIRUDDHA RULE**: `viruddha(H) = 12th from H` (vyaya of a bhava dissolves that bhava).
  Applied **on the primary (first) core bhava only** — resolves adjacency collisions
  (e.g., promotion {10,11}: 12th-from-11 = 10 collides with core; primary-only gives {9}).
- **NEGATIVE QUESTIONS** (job loss, divorce risk, disease onset): the question's native bhavas
  ARE the adverse houses. "Support" there means the adverse event manifests. All such output
  renders through REPORT-01: ~20% weight, *irukkalaam / nadakkalaam / gavanam*, never doom.
- **GENDER-CONDITIONAL KARAKAS** (pending decision #6): Venus = spouse karaka for male chart,
  Jupiter = spouse karaka for female chart. Cards show "Venus/Jupiter*" until locked.
- **FAMILY-MEMBER QUESTIONS**: the relative's bhava becomes the core (4 mother, 9 father,
  7 spouse, 5 child, 3 younger sibling, 11 elder sibling) with 6/8 affliction texture.

# 1. CAREER (thozhil)

> **Domain core**: {2, 6, 10} — Machan's seed. 2 = earnings from work, 6 = service/employment,
> 10 = position/status.
> **Domain karakas**: Saturn (service/labor), Sun (authority/status), Mercury (business/trade).
> **Worked example (sample chart, "promotion in early 2027?")**: Moon bhukti chain = {4,2,7,10}
> → hits core 10 ✓ DBAS. Transit axis sits 4/10 → Ketu transits her 10th ✓ transit.
> Viruddha 9 untouched by either → **POSITIVE**. Deliverer chain runs through exalted Mars
> (Moon's star lord) → the rise is *thagudhi uyarvaana* — boast-worthy (DIGN-01).

### C1 — How is my career overall in this window?
- **Kelvikkana bhavagangal**: core {2, 6, 10} → +11 career with good profits · +3 consulting background & micro-travels · +7 offshore money, offshore partnership, partner/wife involvement · +9 role carries authority/fortune · +5 work becomes creative/intellectual

- **Kelvikkana kaaraka graham**: **Saturn** (job itself) · Sun (status) · Mercury (business)
- **How arrived**: a career is only felt through three channels — what it pays (2), the daily work (6), the position it gives (10). Machan's seed; modifiers are his practice clauses.
