## Part 0 — What the nodes ARE (the doctrine layer)

### 0.1 Chaya graha — the shadow nature

Rahu and Ketu are **chaya grahas** — shadow planets, the two intersection points of the
Moon's orbit with the ecliptic. No body, no light, no sign of their own. Three
consequences the engine depends on:

1. **They own no bhava** → they never appear in the FUNC-01 matrix (functional-nature.md
   Part 1); their functional color is **borrowed per placement/contact** by proxy:
   dispositor → conjunction → aspect, in that priority.
2. **They deliver through hosts** → factor C's dispositor condition IS the node's
   delivery channel. A node is only as strong as its landlord (locked in the worked
   verdicts: transit Rahu in Makaram reads through own-sign yogakaraka Saturn = the
   strongest possible landlord).
3. **They signify by proxy + peculiarity** → SIGN-05: nodes deliver (a) their dispositor's
   portfolio, (b) their occupied house, (c) their star-lord's chain, (d) their peculiar
   karakatvas (grahas.md: Rahu — foreign/mass/amplification/shortcut; Ketu —
   moksha/severance/occult/past-life).

### 0.2 The two heads of one serpent

Rahu = the **head** (desire without stomach): insatiable amplification, foreign pull,
mass appetite, the shortcut, obsession, smoke — it magnifies whatever it touches without
digesting it. Ketu = the **tail** (stomach without head): dissociation, completion,
moksha-vector, past-mastery, the flag (dhvaja), sudden insight, severance — it detaches
from whatever it touches while leaving its essence as wisdom.

**Axis doctrine (locked — "axis-first" framing in AGENTS.md)**: Rahu and Ketu are ONE
axis, 180° apart, always read together. Rahu transiting house H means Ketu transits
H+6: the native is pulled TOWARD Rahu's house themes and pulled AWAY from (or asked to
complete/master) Ketu's house themes, simultaneously, for the full ~18 months. The
report never renders one node alone.

### 0.3 The classical analogies — and their LIMITS

- **"Shanivat Rahu"** — Rahu acts like Saturn: delay, grind, mass-scale, the servant-
  master inversion, chronic pressure. **Limit**: Rahu has no patience; the Saturn
  texture appears in *delivery mechanics* (slow, structural, through systems) while the
  *motivation* stays hot and acquisitive. Never read Rahu as a full Saturn substitute.
- **"Kujavat Ketu"** — Ketu acts like Mars: cuts, heat, sudden events, surgical
  precision, courage. **Limit**: Ketu has no ambition; the Mars texture appears as
  *event mechanics* (sharp, fast, separating) while the *motivation* stays
  renunciatory. Never read Ketu as a full Mars substitute.
- **Engine use**: these analogies license borrowing ONLY for texture clauses and for
  the (excluded) vedha consideration — never for lordship, dignity, or chain math.

### 0.4 Dignity — commentary only (locked exclusion H)

Node exaltation/debilitation signs are school-disputed (Vrishabha/Vrischika,
Mithuna/Dhanus, and other pairs all claimed). **v1: documented, never scored.** The
transit report may carry one commentary line when a node transits a claimed dignity
sign, clearly marked as school-variant — it never enters verdict math.

### 0.5 Aspects — OFF by default (locked)

Node aspects (5/7/9 from the node, school-disputed) are a **toggle, default OFF** in
v1. Contacts are conjunction-only (factor B). The 7th-from axis effect is already
captured by reading the axis as a unit (Part 5) — turning node aspects ON would
double-count the partner house.

### 1.2 Mean vs true node (locked: report mean)

- **Mean node**: smooth, always retrograde — what panchangas print, what Parashari
  gochara uses. **The engine reports this.**
- **True node**: oscillates; stations and brief direct motion occur near eclipses.
  A true-node contact can therefore happen **three times** (retrograde pass, direct
  pass, retrograde pass). Mean-node contact happens **once**.
- **Locked v1 behavior**: compute both, report mean. Every contact = **one contiguous
  window** with one exact date. (v2 option: true-node triple-pass as "echo" notes.)

### 1.3 Contact windows (factor B orb mechanics — ORB-02 asymmetric curve)

Locked curve (Machan, 2026-10-31 — replaces the earlier symmetric ≤3°/≤5° orbs):
influence **begins when the node nears 3° before the natal planet, peaks at the exact
crossing, starts declining the moment it crosses (even by 1'), and is completely gone
2.5° past**. The node walks retrograde, so it approaches from the higher degree:

- **Window start** = natal degree **+ 3°** (applying edge — the rise begins)
- **Exact peak** = natal degree (maximum intensity)
- **Window end** = natal degree **− 2.5°** (separating edge — fully released)
- At 0.05295°/day: **rise ≈ 57 days · fade ≈ 47 days · total ≈ 104 days** per contact.
- **Both readings and timeline language follow the curve**: "approaching" before the
  peak, "at its peak" at exact, "releasing / has released" after.
- **Consequence for stelliums**: consecutive ~104-day windows nearly **abut** rather
  than blur (in the Dec-2026 showcase the Venus window opens ~6 days after the Moon
  window closes) → the engine renders **one continuous contact season with dated
  rise–peak–release markers per contact**, not discrete on/off blocks.
- ⚠️ **Sign-border question (gate-kept, GK-12)**: for a planet in a sign's last 3°,
  the applying phase begins *before* the node enters the sign (sample-chart Moon:
  rise starts ≈ 23 days pre-ingress). Ruling pending: does influence respect the
  sign boundary, or does the rise read from the border-crossing date?

### 1.4 Retrograde contact order

Nodes enter a sign at its **30° end and walk backward to 0°**. Within a sign, contacts
fire in **reverse degree order** (highest natal degree first). The engine sorts natal
grahas in the transited sign by descending longitude and emits the contact list in
that order — no ephemeris search needed beyond the entry date and mean speed.

### 1.5 Nakshatra sub-periods within a sign transit (factor D spine)

Each sign hosts 3 nakshatra segments (2¼ stars). Retrograde traversal order = reverse
star order. Sub-period flavor = **star-lord's nature + portfolio + (for the natal
chart being read) the star-lord's own condition**. Locked intensification: when the
transiting node moves through **stars lorded by itself or its pair** (Rahu: Ardra,
Swathi, Sadayam; Ketu: Ashwini, Magha, Moolam), the sub-period reads one grade louder
(factor D's amplification clause).

## Part 3 — Return & half-return (factor B's self-contacts)

- **Rahu return** (transit Rahu conjoins natal Rahu, every ≈18.6y; ages ~18.6, 37.2,
  55.9, 74.5…): the **desire-cycle resets** — the native re-meets the life-theme the
  natal node axis encodes, one octave up. Major reorientation windows: foreign moves,
  career reinvention, appetite redefinition. The natal Rahu house re-activates at full
  volume; the axis partner (Ketu on natal Ketu) completes the old cycle simultaneously.
- **Half-return** (transit Rahu conjoins natal Ketu / transit Ketu conjoins natal
  Rahu, every ≈9.3y; ages ~9.3, 27.9, 46.5, 65.2…): the **axis flips** — desire meets
  its own tail: what was chased gets questioned; what was renounced calls back. The
  classic "is this what I wanted?" checkpoint. Softer than the return, more
  diagnostic: it reveals rather than rebuilds.
- **Reading rule**: return/half-return color the WHOLE sign-transit they occur in (they
  are slow self-contacts), and stack with every other factor-B contact active in the
  window. They never carry house semantics of their own — the natal node houses supply
  those.
- **Both natal-node-star and natal-node-sign contacts are already covered** by the 14
  templates' cross-rule; return/half-return is the special case where "the contacted
  graha IS the node" — read the natal node's portfolio (house, dispositor, star-lord)
  as the amplified/released content.

## Part 4 — The six axis templates (factor A's reading bank)

*Axis-first doctrine (locked): the transit is read as ONE axis landing on a bhava pair,
from the Moon PRIMARY and from the Lagna SECONDARY (factor A). Locked favorability:
Rahu ✓ in 3/6/10/11 · Ketu ✓ in 3/6/11. Templates below are generic; the engine
re-points them per reference frame and per domain lens.*

### Axis 1/7 — Rahu on self, Ketu on other (or reverse)
- **Rahu-1 / Ketu-7**: identity reinvention, image hunger, self-focused ambition;
  partnerships thin out or spiritualize; the native becomes the event. Marriage lens:
  self-assertion vs union — negotiation needed. Health lens: vitality redirected inward.
- **Rahu-7 / Ketu-1**: partnerships magnetize (often foreign/unconventional), the other
  becomes the obsession; self quiets. Marriage lens: strongest alliance-formation axis;
  the partner arrives larger than life.

### Axis 2/8 — Rahu on vault, Ketu on depth (or reverse)
- **Rahu-2 / Ketu-8**: money appetite surges, speech sharpens, family-of-origin themes
  amplify; chronic/hidden matters simplify by release. Money lens: accumulation drive,
  unconventional income channels open.
- **Rahu-8 / Ketu-2**: the hidden magnetizes — inheritance, insurance, occult, sudden
  events; the vault leaks or detaches (speech/family distance). Money lens: windfall-
  flavored but unstable; health lens: chronic watch.

### Axis 3/9 — Rahu on effort, Ketu on grace (or reverse)
- **Rahu-3 / Ketu-9**: courage and initiative surge; bold moves, media, short travels;
  luck-by-grace quiets into luck-by-hustle (dharma externalized into effort).
  Favorable axis per locked flags (both nodes well-placed). Career lens: self-made
  openings; foreign lens: short journeys active, long-dharma quiet.
- **Rahu-9 / Ketu-3**: fortune, father, foreign, higher-learning magnetize; effortless
  openings appear; initiative by effort cools. Foreign lens: the strongest long-
  journey axis. Ketu in 3rd = the courage of disinterest (acts without attachment).

### Axis 4/10 — Rahu on home, Ketu on career (or reverse) — *the sample chart's axis*
- **Rahu-4 / Ketu-10**: home/property/vehicles/mother-base amplify — moves, purchases,
  renovations, emotional churn at the base; career title detaches or restructures
  quietly. Money lens: asset-building window (earned channel, not windfall). Health
  lens: chest/heart/peace watch. **The showcase: Part 5.**
- **Rahu-10 / Ketu-4**: career magnetizes — visibility, unconventional role, foreign
  employer flavor; home base thins (relocation texture). Career lens: the loudest
  promotion axis; favorable flag applies to Rahu-10.

### Axis 5/11 — Rahu on merit, Ketu on gains (or reverse)
- **Rahu-5 / Ketu-11**: creativity, children, romance, SPECULATION amplify (the gamble
  calls); gains detach into fewer-but-truer channels; elder networks quiet. Money
  lens: speculation watch — the M4-style negative gate lives here when the chart is
  weak; education lens: unconventional study.
- **Rahu-11 / Ketu-5**: gains, networks, ambitions magnetize — income channels widen,
  mass contact, elder-sibling themes; creative/children matters simplify. Money lens:
  strongest gains axis (favorable flag on Rahu-11).

### Axis 6/12 — Rahu on arena, Ketu on release (or reverse)
- **Rahu-6 / Ketu-12**: competition, service, health-of-routine amplify — the native
  out-fights rivals (favorable flag); expenses/foreign-residence quiet or
  spiritualize; litigation wins. Health lens: acute battles won, routine discipline.
- **Rahu-12 / Ketu-6**: expenses, foreign residence, isolation, hospitals/ashrams
  magnetize — the outward-bound axis; enemies dissolve by disinterest. Foreign lens:
  the residence-abroad axis; money lens: expense watch; health lens: hospitalization
  texture (REPORT-01 probabilistic register).
