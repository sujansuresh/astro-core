## How to read a nakshatra entry

Each star carries, in fixed order:

1. **Data line** — span (rasi + degrees), star-lord + Vimshottari dasha length, deity,
   symbol, gana, yoni, nadi, motivation (purushartha), nature class (7-fold muhurta
   classification). Pure data; this is what the engine reads. **Star-lord + span feeds
   SIGN-02/03 and factor D — if it's not here, the signification chain cannot run.**
2. **Texture** — how this star *feels* and delivers.
3. **Good characteristics** (well-placed graha/lagna/Moon in this star) — bullet bank.
4. **Bad characteristics** (afflicted placement here) — bullet bank.
5. **Karakatvas** — numbered signification list (people, matters, objects, places,
   professions). Cite corrections by star + number (e.g., "Rohini #12").
6. **Pada map** — the 4 padas with navamsa rasi + one-line flavor. Feeds pada-precision
   in reports (e.g., sample chart Moon = Avittam pada 2).
7. **Transit note (factor D)** — what a transit graha (esp. the nodes) delivers while
   passing through this star. This is the sub-period flavor bank.
8. **Dasha note (factor E)** — texture of this star-lord's mahadasha.
9. **Worked example** — only for stars occupied in the sample chart (Thiruvathirai,
   Punarvasu… full list in footer).

**Conventions**: navamsa padas computed by the standard rule (movable rasis start navamsa
from themselves, fixed from the 9th, dual from the 5th). Tamil names alongside Sanskrit.
Where parambariya schools disagree, the item is tagged **[school note]** for Machan's
ruling.

**Nature classes (7-fold muhurta classification)** — Sthira (fixed), Chara (movable),
Laghu-Kshipra (light-swift), Mridu (soft), Madhya/Misra (mixed), Ugra-Krura (fierce),
Tikshna-Daruna (sharp-dreadful). Feeds the texture axis of factor D.

**Vimshottari lengths**: Ketu 7 · Venus 20 · Sun 6 · Moon 10 · Mars 7 · Rahu 18 ·
Jupiter 16 · Saturn 19 · Mercury 17 (total 120).

**Transit (factor D)**: node/graha crossing Ashwini = sudden initiations, medical events

**Dasha note (factor E)**: Ketu MD born under Ashwini opens with dissolution-then-healing;
